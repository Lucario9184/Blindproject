HOW SANKETSETU WORKS

5-Stage Real-Time ISL Translation Architecture

From live camera hand tracking to 22-language regional voice synthesis and 3D avatar signing.

Stage 01

3D Hand & Pose Tracking

Extracts 21 3D hand landmarks per frame from the webcam with palm-scale normalization and motion smoothing.

Stage 02

Sign Gesture Classifier

Matches finger extension angles, thumb-finger pinch geometry, and two-handed postures across 5,000+ ISL signs.

Stage 03

ISL Grammar Decoder

Converts continuous ISL gloss tokens into natural sentences across Hindi, Maithili, Tamil, Bengali, and 18+ regional languages.

Stage 04

Regional Human Voice

Speaks the translated sentence aloud using 24kHz native regional human voice recordings across 22 Indian languages.

Stage 05

3D Signing Avatar

Translates regional speech and text back into animated two-handed ISL gestures on our 3D Signing Avatar.

BHASHINI DIGITAL INCLUSION FOR DEAF INDIA

Who SanketSetu Serves Across India

Inspired by the Government of India's Bhashini mission to eliminate language barriers for non-English speakers, rural citizens, and the Deaf community.

1. General Deaf Citizens & Rural Families
22Languages

Non-English Speakers & Low Literacy: Allows Deaf individuals and their families who only speak regional languages (Hindi, Maithili, Bhojpuri, Tamil, Bengali, Punjabi, Odia) to communicate through sign-to-voice and voice-to-avatar in hospitals, railways, schools, and rural public services.

2. Schools, Clinics & Public Service Builders
2-WayBridge

Accessible Digital Services: Helps hospitals, classrooms, banks, and local businesses communicate directly with Deaf citizens in their local state language without needing a full-time interpreter on site.

3. "Bhasha Daan" Sign Contributors
CustomSigns

Community Sign Training: Deaf signers, teachers, and volunteers can record and calibrate local regional ISL signs directly using the built-in Custom Sign Trainer to expand vocabulary across all Indian states.
