🎙️ Available Voice Commands (Press Space or Alt + V)

Speak any of these phrases after activating the Voice Command microphone:

  • "Camera" or "Sign to speech" — Opens Tab 1 (Webcam ISL Reader)
  • "Translate" or "Speech to sign" — Opens Tab 2 (Voice to ISL Visuals)
  • "Dictionary" or "Learn" — Opens Tab 3 (Tactile Audio ISL Dictionary)
  • "Quick board" or "Assist" — Opens Tab 4 (Quick Communication Board)
  • "Add sign" or "Train sign" — Opens the Custom ISL Sign Calibration Studio
  • "How to sign [word]" (e.g., "How to sign thank you") — Speaks the tactile hand instructions for that sign
  • "Translate [sentence]" (e.g., "Translate please help me") — Translates and displays the sentence in ISL
  • "High contrast" — Toggles Ultra High-Contrast Yellow-on-Black theme
  • "Emergency" or "Help" — Triggers the audio-visual SOS alert

⌨️ Eyes-Free Keyboard Shortcuts

Navigate the entire platform without a mouse or screen visibility:

  • 1, 2, 3, 4 — Switch directly between the 4 main modes
  • Space — Activate Voice Command Microphone (when not typing in a text box)
  • H — Hear spoken Orientation & Help Guide at any time
  • C — Toggle Ultra High-Contrast Yellow-on-Black visual theme
  • S — Speak current detected sign or active stage summary aloud
  • Escape — Stop any ongoing speech or close modal dialogs
